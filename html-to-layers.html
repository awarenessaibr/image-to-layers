Pipeline

Each .stage block becomes one project. Text layers keep their CSS position, colour, font and rotation, and fade in and out from data-s / data-e. Background photos come from --img, background-image or <img>; other images become their own layers. Segmentation splits the background into named cut-outs behind the text. Amber outlines mark layers whose position was estimated rather than read from CSS.
