Heritage Collection · places reels

Three different 9:16 reels built on the book locations: a camera flight over the world map, a heritage passport and a guess-the-country quiz.

Notes: Maps and country shapes are drawn from public Natural Earth data (110m, simplified). Pins mark the regions on the covers: Rio Grande do Sul, Piemonte, Cornwall (Tintagel) and Kraków; Chile is pinned loosely because the cover shows no region, and its title is still a placeholder. Coordinates are approximate. Copy lives in the C object at the top of the script.
